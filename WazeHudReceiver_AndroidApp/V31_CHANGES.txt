HUD Receiver V31

- Removed the visible white mirror/flip circle.
- Navigation header, current-speed block, and speed-limit/alert block are independently editable.
- Touch an element to display its white rounded outline.
- Drag with one finger to reposition it.
- Pinch with two fingers to resize it without rotation.
- Position and scale are saved automatically when the last finger is released.
- Saved layout is restored after restarting the application.
- Scale range: 35% to 250%.
- Based on the V30.1 networking/header-fixed receiver.
